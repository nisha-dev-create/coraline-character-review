Coraline Character Review

This is a character reveiw on Coraline:

Character Card

  • Name: Coraline Jones
  • Age: 11 years old
  • Parents Names: Mel and Charlie Jones
  • Appearances: Neil Gaiman's 2002 novella and the 2009 animated film by Laika Studios.
  • Nationality: She was British in the original children's book by Neil Gaiman and American in the Laika film.

Coraline's Key CHaracter Traits

  • Curiosity: Her intense desire to explore her new, rambling environment drives the entire plot, leading her to unlock the secret door to the Other World.
  • Bravery: Though she is small for her age and deeply frightened, she confronts the manipulative Beldam (Other Mother) to rescue her real parents and the trapped souls of ghost children.
  • Wits and Resourcefulness: She relies on quick thinking, logic, and sharp intuition—rather than brute strength—to solve the Other Mother's games and escape the trap.
  • Stubbornness and Feistiness: She is strong-willed, outspoken, and frequently annoyed by the rain, boring food, or adults who dismiss her because of her youth.
  • Independence and Loneliness: As an only child often ignored by her overworked parents, she is used to entertaining herself, which initially makes her vulnerable to the Other Mother's deceptive illusions of attention.
  • Growth and Appreciation: Over the course of her trial, she moves past her initial bitterness and learns to value the imperfect, real-world love of her parents as well as the value of true companionship.
